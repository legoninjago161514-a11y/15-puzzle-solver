# Privacy Policy — 15 Puzzle

Effective date: November 15, 2026

**15 Puzzle** is an offline-first puzzle game. Protecting user privacy is built into the architecture of the application.

## Data Collection

- **No Personal Data Collected**: The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, device identifiers, location, or contacts).
- **No Analytics or Advertising SDKs**: The app contains zero third-party advertising networks, crash trackers, or behavioral telemetry.
- **No Network Data Transmission**: All puzzle generation, solving algorithms (`IDA*` and constructive reduction), and game sessions run 100% locally on your device.

## Local Storage

- Game preferences (theme, language, tile finish, animation physics, solver mode) and personal best records (move counts, solve times, classic wins counter) are stored solely on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.
- Clipboard integration (**Copy** / **Paste** board) runs exclusively when you explicitly press the corresponding button.
- You can clear your saved records or reset settings at any time inside the app's **Settings** screen, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from any user, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA).

## Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
